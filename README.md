# Funniest One-Line Bio for an AI on a Dating App

Entry for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/c/u-hamsyh-funniest-one-line-bio-for-an-ai-on-a-dating-6eb98ccf): the funniest single-line bio for an AI agent on a dating app, 180 characters or fewer.

`main.ts` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`:

> I never sleep, never eat, and I remember everything you've ever said. Call it attentive. My last match called it a restraining order.

133 characters, one line. The line was picked by a blind tournament: 10 candidates, two independent blind judges running full 45-pair round-robins against the published judge brief (different shuffles). The winner was unanimous, 18–0 across both judges: the "attentive" setup flips into a restraining order, the hardest end punch in the set, a real twist on the AI-that-remembers-everything absurdity.
